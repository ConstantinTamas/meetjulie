Privacy · Updated 11 September 2026

Your enquiry stays a conversation.

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit meetjulie.org or contact us through the website.

Who is responsible

Tamás Constantin is the data controller, acting as a private individual in Hungary. You can contact him through the contact form or by replying to correspondence you receive from him.

What you choose to send

The contact form asks for your name and email address so that we can reply. Your institution, role and message are optional. Please do not send patient information, medical records or other sensitive personal data.

Your details are used to read and answer your enquiry. They are not used for a mailing list, advertising, profiling or automated decisions. Processing your enquiry is based on the consent you give when submitting the form, under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR.

Hosting, delivery and security

The website is hosted by Tárhely.Eu in Hungary. Hosting and email service providers process the information needed to operate the website and deliver correspondence. They receive it for those purposes; it is not sold or shared for marketing.

The website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or remotely loaded fonts. When you use the contact form, an essential session cookie helps protect your submission. It expires at the end of the browser session. Technical security information, including server access logs and pseudonymous rate-limit counters, helps prevent abuse. That processing is based on the legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

How long information is kept

Enquiries are deleted when the correspondence is closed or after twelve months, whichever comes first. Short-lived form sessions and rate-limit counters are used separately from correspondence. Hosting logs and backup copies follow the service providers' retention arrangements and are not used for marketing.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, request restriction or portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Use the contact form or reply to our correspondence to make a request. We may need to confirm that the request relates to your information before acting on it. If you withdraw consent, we will stop using your details to continue the enquiry.

You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).
